Las reglas
1–12 palabras propias: one, two, three… twelve
13–19 terminan en -teen: thirteen, fourteen…
20,30,40… terminan en -ty: twenty, thirty, forty…
Mezcla decena + guion + unidad: 47 = forty-seven
⚠️ Ojo: thirteen (13) vs thirty (30) — ¡suenan parecido!
El verbo "to be" en pasado
was I · he · she · it  →  una sola cosa o persona
were you · we · they  →  varias, y siempre con "you"
💡 Toca una frase para escucharla. Mantén pulsado para oírla despacio 🐢
Negativo y pregunta
was not = wasn't — She wasn't at home.
were not = weren't — They weren't happy.
¿? El verbo va delante: Was he tired? · Were you there?
💡 Toca una letra para escucharla. Mantén pulsado para oírla despacio 🐢
Las más difíciles
G / J G = "yi"  ·  J = "yei" — ¡no las confundas!
E / I E = "i"  ·  I = "ai"
W se dice "dábliu" (double u = doble u)
Y se dice "uai", parecido a "why"
